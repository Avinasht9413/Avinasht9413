## 🚀 What I'm Currently Building

### 🤖 Agentic AI Hiring Automation Platform

My capstone project focuses on building an AI-powered hiring platform that helps HR teams automate **job description generation, resume processing, candidate evaluation, and shortlisting**.

The platform uses **RAG and company-specific knowledge** to generate job descriptions, provides human approval gates for important hiring decisions, and automatically communicates with shortlisted candidates after HR approval.

### Key Workflow

1. HR logs in through authentication.
2. AI generates a job description using RAG and previous company JDs.
3. HR reviews and approves the JD.
4. The platform publishes the JD through a public application portal.
5. Candidates submit their resumes through the portal.
6. AI processes and filters resumes based on the approved requirements.
7. HR reviews the shortlisted candidates.
8. After approval, the platform sends emails to shortlisted candidates.
